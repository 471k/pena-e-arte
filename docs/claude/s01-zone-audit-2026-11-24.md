# S01 zone audit: the six `withStudioTimeZone` sites and the 61 `toLocale*` files — 2026-11-24

- **From:** Claude Code, at `main` = `40d316ad` (after #444). Desktop's 24 Nov instruction §D.
- **The rule** (now also written above `formatStudioTime` in `shared/utils/formatInStudioTimezone.ts`):
  **a date that locates something in the studio's operating day follows the studio's zone; a date that records
  when something happened follows the reader's.** A date used to put money into a studio-calendar period counts as
  the first kind. That is why MyEarnings' "Paid" and the payment list's paid date are studio-zone.
- **A third kind turned up** that the rule doesn't cover: **date-only values**, which follow neither zone (a date
  of birth, a closure day, a tattoo record's date). The API stores them as UTC midnight (`UtcDateTimeConverter`), so
  anywhere west of UTC the viewer's zone prints the day before. They now go through `formatDateOnly`, which pins UTC.

## 3. S01's verdict

**S01 stays Partial.** The model (`formatStudioTime` + `useStudioZone`, no browser fallback) exists, but it is not
applied everywhere a studio-day date is shown:

- **Fixed in this PR:** ArtistDetailPage's bookings tab and ClientDetailPage's appointments tab.
- **Phase E of this run:** PaymentDetailPage, PaymentListPage, UnsettledPaymentsSection, MyEarningsPage and
  NoShowConfirmDialog (the money pages).
- **Not fixed, needs a backend field:** the review and conduct-report visit pickers. `ReviewableAppointmentResponse`
  and `ReportableAppointmentResponse` carry no studio zone.
- **Undecided:** booth rent's "Charged" date, in two files. It is studio-zone if it is reconciled into a studio
  period, and nothing in the frontend says whether it is.

S01 becomes Present when phase E lands, the two responses carry the zone, and booth rent is decided.
