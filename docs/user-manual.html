Pena e Arte — Complete User Manual

Everything you need to know about booking, managing, and administering a tattoo studio on the platform. Use the sidebar to jump to any feature, or use the role filter above to see only what applies to you.

🗺Overview & Roles ▶
All roles

Pena e Arte is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for tattoo studios. Each studio is a completely isolated tenant — clients, artists, bookings, designs, and payments are never shared between studios. Four roles exist:

RoleWhoDefault landing pageCapabilities summary
Client The person getting tattooed /book Book appointments, pay deposits, review designs, sign consent forms, manage own profile
Artist Tattoo artist at a studio /schedule View schedule, upload design revisions, manage clients, confirm cash deposits
Owner Studio owner / manager /dashboard Full studio management: clients, artists, designs, payments, billing, settings
Issuer Platform administrator /platform Cross-tenant oversight: studios, subscriptions, plans, referrals, analytics
ℹ️
Every studio gets a 14-day free trial with full access — no credit card required at signup. After the trial there is a 7-day read-only grace period, then the studio must subscribe to continue taking bookings.

Data Isolation

Every database query is automatically scoped to the current studio (tenant). You can never accidentally see another studio's clients, appointments, or payments. The only exceptions are the public portfolio pages, the public studio map, and (with explicit opt-in) the client portable profile feature.

🔐Authentication ▶
All roles

Logging In

  1. Navigate to the platform URL. You'll land on /login.

  2. Enter your email address and password.

  3. Click Sign in. The system reads your role from the JWT token and redirects you automatically to the correct dashboard for your role.

Forgot Password

  1. On the login page, click Forgot password?

  2. Enter your email address and click Send reset link.

  3. Check your email. Click the link — it takes you to /reset-password.

  4. Enter your new password twice and confirm. You'll be redirected to login.

⚠️
Password reset links expire after a short window. If your link has expired, simply request a new one from the forgot-password page.

Studio Registration (Owner)

  1. Visit the registration page (your platform's /register route).

  2. Fill in studio name, your email, and choose a password.

  3. If you received a referral code from another studio owner, enter it in the referral code field — this unlocks a discount on your first paid month.

  4. Complete registration. A 14-day trial starts immediately.

How Sessions Work

The platform uses short-lived JWT tokens. Your session is kept alive automatically while you're actively using the app. If you're inactive for an extended period you'll be asked to log in again — your data is never lost.

📅Booking an Appointment ▶
Client

Clients book appointments from the Book page (/book). The booking widget is embedded in the studio's interface.

Step-by-step

  1. Log in. You land on the Book an Appointment page.

  2. Select the Artist you want from the dropdown. Artists who have been added by the studio owner will appear here.

  3. Pick a Date and time for your appointment. The calendar only allows future dates.

  4. Set the expected Duration (30 minutes to 8 hours in 30-minute increments).

  5. Add any optional notes for the artist (e.g. reference image description, placement preference).

  6. Submit the form. The system checks that the selected slot is not already booked — if it is, you'll see a "slot already taken" error and can choose another time.

  7. After a successful booking, you'll be taken to the Deposit Payment step automatically.

Example

Maria wants a sleeve tattoo from artist João. She picks Saturday 14:00 for 4 hours, writes "Black and grey geometric sleeve, full upper arm" in the notes, and submits. The system creates a Pending appointment and redirects her to pay the deposit.

Cancelling a Booking

From My Bookings, find the appointment you wish to cancel and click Cancel appointment. Cancellation policies (whether the deposit is refunded) are set by the studio via Deposit Rules — check the studio's policy before cancelling.

💳Deposits & Payment ▶
Client Owner Artist

Every booking requires a deposit before it is confirmed. The deposit amount and rules are configured by the studio owner. Two payment methods are available:

💳 Card (Stripe)

Pay online with any debit or credit card (Visa, Mastercard, etc.) via Stripe's secure Payment Element. Your card is authorised (held) now, then captured when your session is complete — you are not charged until the appointment is finished.

💵 Cash

Select Cash to declare you'll pay in person at the studio. This creates a pending record. The studio owner or artist must physically confirm receipt of cash before your appointment is marked as deposit-paid.

🎨Design Approval Workflow ▶
Client Artist Owner

Designs follow a collaborative revision loop between artist and client. Artists upload design drafts and clients provide feedback before the final tattoo is applied.

Full Workflow

1. Owner or Artist creates a Design record (links it to a client)
2. Artist uploads Revision v1 (image file) with optional notes
3. Client is notified — opens the Design and sees the image
4a. Client clicks Approve → status becomes Approved
4b. Client clicks Request Changes + optional notes → status becomes Changes Requested
5. Artist uploads Revision v2 addressing the feedback
6. Repeat until Approved — each version is stored and visible

Client: How to Review a Design

  1. Navigate to Designs in the top menu.

  2. Find the design linked to your appointment and click it.

  3. The latest revision image appears. Scroll down to see earlier versions.

  4. Click Approve if you're happy — a green "Approved" badge appears and the artist is notified.

  5. Or click Request Changes, type your feedback in the text field (e.g. "Please make the roses larger and add more shading around the stem"), then click Submit.

Artist: How to Upload a Revision

  1. Go to Designs → click the design → click Upload Revision in the top-right corner.

  2. Select your image file (JPEG, PNG, SVG accepted).

  3. Add optional notes describing what changed in this version.

  4. Click Upload. The image is stored securely in Cloudflare R2. The client receives an in-app and email notification.

Example

Artist Rui uploads a dragon design sketch (v1) for client Pedro. Pedro opens the design, sees the sketch, and requests changes: "The wings should be wider and I'd like a moon in the background." Rui revises the design and uploads v2. Pedro reviews v2, is satisfied, and clicks Approve. The design is locked and the artist proceeds to tattoo.

ℹ️
All revision versions are permanently stored. You can always go back and view earlier drafts. The artist can delete individual revisions if needed.
👤Client Profile & Body Map ▶
Client Artist Owner

Each client has a rich profile that stores their tattoo history and body map. This gives artists quick context before a session.

Body Map

The interactive body map is an SVG diagram of a human figure split into 20+ named zones on the front and 22+ zones on the back. Artists and clients can click zones to mark where tattoos have been placed or are planned.

  • Front zones include: Head, Neck, Chest, Abdomen, Left/Right Shoulder, Upper Arm, Forearm, Hand, Thigh, Knee, Shin, Foot
  • Back zones include: Skull, Neck (back), Upper Back, Lower Back, Buttock, Calf, Heel — and mirrored arm zones

How to update the body map (Client)

  1. Go to My Profile.

  2. Scroll to the Body Map section. The SVG figure is interactive.

  3. Toggle between Front and Back views using the tab buttons.

  4. Click any body zone to highlight it (primary colour = selected, muted = empty).

  5. Selected zones appear as chips below the figure for easy reading.

  6. Changes are saved automatically.

Tattoo History

Every completed appointment generates a Tattoo Record in the client's history. Artists can attach photos of completed work to each record. Clients can view — but not edit — their tattoo history.

Example

Client Beatriz has tattoos on her Left Upper Arm and Right Shin. Her body map has these two zones highlighted. When artist Tiago opens her profile, he can immediately see the placement history. He links her new appointment (left forearm sleeve) to a tattoo record with a photo after the session.

📝Consent & Intake Forms ▶
Client Artist Owner

Intake Forms

An Intake Form collects health and preference information from the client before the session. Studios use these to check for contraindications (allergies, medications, skin conditions).

  • Clients complete intake forms linked to a specific appointment.
  • Owners and artists can view submitted intake forms from the Forms section.

Consent Forms

A Consent Form is a legal document the client signs before tattooing begins. The platform captures a digital signature and timestamps it.

Consent Form Workflow

  1. Studio creates/sends a consent form linked to the client's appointment.

  2. Client receives a notification and opens the form.

  3. Client reviews the consent text, types their signature in the signature field, and submits.

  4. On submission, the system generates a PDF of the signed consent form and stores it in Cloudflare R2.

  5. Owner and artist can download the PDF from the Consent Forms list at any time.

What the consent form PDF contains

Client name, appointment date, digital signature (as typed), timestamp of signing, and the studio's consent text. If the studio is on a paid plan with branding removal disabled, the Pena e Arte badge appears in the footer.

For Owners: Viewing Forms

  1. Go to Forms → Consent Forms or Intake Forms.

  2. Each form shows its status: Pending (not yet signed) or Signed.

  3. Click a consent form to see the signature, signing timestamp, and a link to download the PDF.

🔗Portable Profile (Cross-Studio) ▶
Client

By default, your client profile is private to the studio where it was created. The Portable Profile feature lets you opt in to share your basic profile information with other studios on the platform — useful if you visit multiple tattoo studios and want each to see your tattoo history.

⚠️
This feature is opt-in only. Your profile is never shared without your explicit consent. Payment history and consent forms are never included in the portable profile.

How to enable

  1. Go to My Profile.

  2. Find the Portable Profile toggle card.

  3. Toggle it on. A confirmation prompt explains what data will be shared (name, body map, tattoo history — no payments, no forms).

  4. Save. Other studios can now look you up by your user ID if they are adding you as a new client.

How to disable

Toggle the same switch off. Your profile becomes private immediately. Any studio that had already loaded your profile can no longer refresh it.

📏Deposit Rules ▶
Owner

Deposit Rules define how much deposit clients must pay at booking, and what happens to deposits if appointments are cancelled or clients don't show up.

Creating a Deposit Rule

  1. Go to Deposit Rules (/deposit-rules) → click New Rule.

  2. Give the rule a name (e.g. "Standard Deposit", "Weekend Surcharge").

  3. Set the deposit amount (fixed value, e.g. €50) or percentage of session cost.

  4. Configure cancellation policy: how many hours before the appointment a client can cancel and receive a refund.

  5. Save. Rules can be assigned to individual artists or applied studio-wide.

Rule detail view

Each rule shows which artists it is applied to, when it was created, and its current settings. You can edit or delete a rule from the detail view.

Example

Studio "Ink & Soul" has two rules: "Standard" (€50 deposit, free cancellation up to 48h before) and "Short Notice" (€80 deposit, no refund if cancelled within 24h). Weekend appointments use the Short Notice rule automatically.

💰Payments & Session Splits ▶
Owner Artist

Payment List

All studio payments are accessible from /payments. Each payment record shows:

  • Client name, amount, payment method (Card / Cash)
  • Status: Pending / Authorised / CashPending / Paid / Failed / Refunded
  • Date created and paid
  • Link to the associated appointment

Session Splits

For multi-session projects (e.g. a full sleeve done across 4 sittings), the owner can split a payment into labelled installments using the Session Splits Editor.

How to add session splits

  1. Open a payment record.

  2. In the Session Splits section, click Add Splits.

  3. Click + Add Split for each session: enter a label (e.g. "Session 1 — outline") and the amount.

  4. Repeat for all sessions. The labels and amounts are saved.

  5. As each session is paid, mark it as paid — the timestamp is recorded.

Example

Client Diogo commissions a back piece worth €1200. Owner creates 4 splits: "Session 1 — outline €300", "Session 2 — shading €300", "Session 3 — colour €300", "Session 4 — touch-up €300". As each session is completed, the corresponding split is marked paid, giving a clear audit trail.

Generating a Receipt (PDF)

From any completed payment, click Download Receipt. A PDF receipt is generated and downloaded instantly. The receipt includes studio details, client name, amount, date, and payment method.

💵Cash Deposit Confirmation ▶
Owner Artist

When a client chooses to pay their deposit in cash, the appointment shows as CashPending until physically confirmed by a studio member.

Confirming from the Dashboard

The Awaiting Cash section on the dashboard shows all pending cash deposits. For each one:

  1. Find the client's row in the "Awaiting Cash" card on the dashboard.

  2. Click the Confirm button next to their name and amount.

  3. A confirmation dialog shows the client name and amount — click Yes, confirm.

  4. The deposit status changes to Paid and the appointment is fully confirmed.

Confirming from the Payment record

Alternatively, go to Payments, find the CashPending record, and use the confirm button on the payment detail page. The confirming user's ID is recorded on the payment for auditability.

⚠️
Only confirm cash deposits when you have physically received the money. This action is recorded and timestamped. It cannot be undone without manually editing the payment status.

🎁Referral Codes (Studio Owner) ▶
Owner

Owners can generate referral codes to share with other studio owners. When a new studio signs up using your code, they get a discount on their first paid month — and you grow the network.

Generating a referral code

  1. Go to Studio Settings → Referral Codes or navigate to /studios/me and find the Referral section.

💳Billing & Subscriptions (Owner) ▶
Owner

The Billing page (/billing) shows your current subscription status and lets you manage your plan.

Subscribing (Card)

  1. Click Subscribe on the Billing page or dashboard banner.

  2. Choose a plan — Monthly or Yearly. Yearly saves ~17% (equivalent to 2 months free).

  3. Complete payment via Stripe Checkout.

  4. You're redirected back to the billing page — status updates to Active within seconds.

Cash billing

If you cannot pay by card, contact the platform (issuer) out-of-band. The issuer will activate your subscription manually after confirming your cash payment. Your billing page will show "Cash-billed subscription" with the current period end date. You can switch to card billing at any time from the billing page.

Scheduled plan changes (downgrades)

When you downgrade to a cheaper plan, the change takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you don't lose any days you've paid for. The billing page shows a "Scheduled plan change" card with a countdown. You can cancel the change and keep your current plan by clicking Keep current plan.

Suspended by platform

If your studio is suspended by the platform administrator (issuer), a red banner appears at the top of the billing page. Your subscription continues to be charged, but your studio is not accessible to clients. Contact platform support to resolve the suspension.

🏢Studio Oversight (Issuer) ▶
Issuer

The Studios page (/platform/studios) shows every registered studio on the platform. The issuer can search, filter, and take administrative actions on any studio.

Search and filters

  • Search by studio name or slug
  • Filter by status: All, Active, Trialing, GracePeriod, PastDue, Cancelled, NoSubscription, Suspended

Studio card information

Each studio card shows: name, slug, status badge, city, registration date, current plan name, trial/period expiry date.

Available actions per studio

ActionWhen availableWhat it does
Extend trial Studio not on Active subscription Adds N additional days to the trial (max 90). Enter the number of days in the inline form and confirm.
Activate (cash) Studio is NoSubscription / PastDue / GracePeriod / Cancelled Manually activates a subscription after a cash payment is confirmed out-of-band. Select the plan and optionally add a note (e.g. "Cash paid in person 2026-06-01").
Cancel subscription Studio has Active/PastDue/Trialing/GracePeriod subscription Cancels the subscription immediately. Studio enters read-only then suspended state.
Suspend studio Studio is active (isActive = true) Immediately blocks studio access. Billing is not affected — subscription continues to be charged. Use for ToS violations.
Reactivate studio Studio is suspended Restores studio access. The studio owner sees no interruption to their subscription.
Example — Cash activation

Studio "Artbody Lisbon" emailed the platform to pay for a yearly plan via bank transfer. The issuer confirms receipt, opens the studio in the oversight page, clicks Activate (cash), selects "Professional Yearly" plan, adds note "Bank transfer received 2026-06-01 ref: ARTBODY-2026", and clicks Activate. The studio now has an active subscription valid until 2027-06-01.

Example — Trial extension

A studio is in GracePeriod — their trial expired but they haven't subscribed yet. The owner emailed saying they need 2 more weeks to decide. The issuer clicks Extend trial, enters 14 days, confirms. The studio is back to Trialing for 14 more days, with full access restored.

🗂Subscription Oversight (Issuer) ▶
Issuer

The Subscriptions page (/platform/subscriptions) provides a cross-tenant view of all subscription records — every studio's plan, status, and billing dates in one place.

What the table shows

  • Studio name, plan name, billing interval (Monthly/Yearly)
  • Status badge (Active, Trialing, GracePeriod, PastDue, Cancelled)
  • Trial expiry date, current period end date
  • Whether it's card-billed (Stripe) or cash-billed (manual)
  • Stripe subscription ID (for card-billed subs — for support lookups)

Actions available from this page

Same as Studio Oversight: Extend trial, Activate (cash), Cancel subscription. The subscription page provides a billing-focused view while the Studio list provides a studio-focused view — both have the same action set.

ℹ️
Cash-billed subscriptions show "Active until [date]" instead of "Renews [date]" because they do not auto-renew. The issuer must re-activate them each period after confirming payment.
📦Plan Management (Issuer) ▶
Issuer

Plans define what studio owners can subscribe to. Plans are managed at /platform/plans. They are platform-level — not tied to any studio.

Plan fields

FieldDescription
NameDisplay name (e.g. "Starter", "Professional", "Enterprise")
Billing intervalMonthly or Yearly
Price (monthly)Monthly price in EUR
Price (yearly)Yearly price — typically Monthly × 10 (2 months free)
Yearly discount %Displayed to studios on the subscribe page (default: 17%)
Allow Branding RemovalToggle — if true, studios on this plan can disable the Pena e Arte badge

Creating a plan

  1. Go to Plans → click New Plan.

  2. Fill in the name and pricing fields.

  3. Set Allow Branding Removal if this is a paid plan where studios should be able to white-label.

  4. Save. The plan immediately appears on the subscribe page for all studios.

Editing a plan

Click any plan to edit it. Changes to price affect new subscriptions only — existing subscribers keep their current price until they switch plans. The Allow Branding Removal flag takes effect immediately for all studios on this plan.

⚠️
Deleting a plan that has active subscribers is blocked. You must migrate subscribers to another plan first, or cancel their subscriptions.
🎁Referral Code Management (Issuer) ▶
Issuer

The Referrals page (/platform/referrals) gives the issuer a cross-tenant view of every referral code created by every studio, along with redemption counts and the ability to deactivate abusive codes.

What's shown per code

  • The studio that generated the code
  • Code value (8-char uppercase)
  • Created date, expiry date (if set)
  • Active / Inactive status
  • Total redemption count

Deactivating a code

If a referral code is being abused (e.g. shared publicly for mass exploitation of the discount), the issuer can click Deactivate on any code. The code becomes immediately invalid — any new studio trying to use it during registration will see an error. Existing redemptions are not reversed.

Example

Studio "X" shared their referral code in a public forum and it has been redeemed 50 times in 2 days. The issuer flags this as abuse, deactivates the code. The studio can generate a new code for legitimate referrals.

📈Industry Analytics Reports (Issuer) ▶
Issuer

Monthly industry reports are automatically generated on the 1st of each month by a background job. They contain aggregated, anonymised metrics across all studios — no PII, no studio names.

Report contents

MetricDescription
Avg appointments/monthPlatform-wide average across all active studios
Peak booking hoursMost popular times of day for bookings
Top session durationsDistribution of 1h, 2h, 3h+ sessions
Retention rate% of clients who book more than once
Trial-to-paid conversion% of trial studios that subscribed within 30 days
🔒
Reports only show aggregated data with a minimum cohort size of 10 studios. No individual studio or client can be identified from a report. All data is stored as anonymised JSON in Cloudflare R2.

Accessing reports

  1. Go to Reports (/platform/reports).

  2. A list of available monthly reports is shown — one per month going back to the platform's first month.

  3. Click a month to download the JSON report or view the formatted summary.

🗺Studio Map (Public) ▶
Public — no login required

The Studio Map (/map) is publicly accessible with no login required. It shows all active studios on the platform on an interactive map.

What it shows

  • Map pins for every active studio with a configured location
  • Click a pin: studio name, city, and a link to their public portfolio page
  • No private information is ever shown — only name, slug, and city

For studio owners

To appear on the map, make sure your studio's latitude and longitude are set in Studio Settings. Suspended or inactive studios are excluded from the map automatically.

🖼Public Portfolio Pages ▶
Public — no login required

Every active studio and every artist gets a public portfolio page with a permanent, SEO-friendly URL. No login is needed to view them.

Studio portfolio

URL: https://penaearte.com/s/{studio-slug}

  • Studio name, bio, city
  • List of artists with links to their individual portfolios
  • Booking CTA button (links to the booking page)
  • Platform branding badge (unless the studio has removed it on a paid plan)

Artist portfolio

URL: https://penaearte.com/artist/{artist-slug}

  • Artist name, bio, specialties
  • Portfolio gallery (approved design images)
  • Link back to the studio

Design share page

URL: https://penaearte.com/share/{token}

Time-limited, revocable. Shows only the design image and title — no client or studio identifiers. Useful for clients sharing designs for feedback before approving.

ℹ️
Portfolio pages are indexed by Google. Studios benefit from organic SEO — clients searching for tattoo studios in their city can discover the studio through Google without needing a paid ad.
