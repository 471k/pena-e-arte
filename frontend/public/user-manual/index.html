TattooOS — User Manual

TattooOS is a booking and studio-management platform for tattoo studios. A studio signs up once and runs its whole business through it — appointments, deposits, digital consent and intake forms, design approval, client records, and payments — while visitors discover artists and book sessions online.

The five roles

The app has five kinds of user, and what they see depends entirely on who they are:

  • Guest — anyone browsing without an account. Can discover studios and artists, view portfolios, read reviews, and start a booking or sign-up.
  • Client — a person getting tattooed. Books appointments, fills in forms, approves designs, tracks their tattoo history.
  • Artist — a tattooer working at a studio. Manages their own schedule, clients, and designs.
  • Owner — the studio's manager/admin. Everything an artist can do, plus staff, pricing, payments, and studio settings.
  • Issuer — TattooOS's own platform team. Oversees every studio, subscription plans, and platform-wide health — not tied to any one studio.

A client can belong to more than one studio at a time and switch between them from My Studios. Everyone else belongs to exactly one studio.

How to use this manual

Pick a role tab in the left sidebar to see that role's screens, grouped by feature area. Click any link to jump straight to that screen's walkthrough, or press Ctrl+K to search the whole manual by keyword. Each screen entry shows who can access it, a simple wireframe of the layout, and numbered steps in the exact order the fields and buttons appear on screen. A Reference tab at the end has status glossaries and a role comparison table.

Platform URLs at a glance

Public pages live at the root of the site (e.g. /discover, /s/studio-slug). Signed-in pages sit behind role checks — visiting a page your role can't use redirects you to your own home screen automatically.

The homepage for anyone browsing the platform, at /discover. It detects the visitor's location (or falls back to a default city), and shows either a scrollable feed of tattoo photos or a grid of nearby studios, switchable with a tab. Signed-in visitors see their account menu instead of sign-in links.

Steps

  1. Location resolves automatically — the browser is asked for permission; if denied, the feed defaults to Lisbon, Portugal.
  2. Search a city in the search box to look up a different location instead.
  3. Switch tabs between Portfolio (photo feed) and Studios (grid of nearby studios with ratings, distance, and artist count).
  4. Filter by style using the chip row (Blackwork, Realism, Traditional, etc.) — Portfolio tab only.
  5. Toggle "Near me" to widen the search beyond your immediate area.
  6. Click a photo to open it full-size with reviews and a "Book with [artist]" button; click a studio card to open its full profile.
  7. Bookmark photos (signed-in users only) by hovering a tile and clicking the bookmark icon.
Tip: the Studios tab has a radius selector (10/25/50/100 km) for widening or narrowing your search area.
See also: Studio profile page, Artist profile page, Studio map

A simple two-step password reset flow: request a reset link by email at /forgot-password, then use the token from that email on /reset-password to set a new password. Reset links expire 1 hour after they're sent.

Steps

  1. Enter your Email and click Send reset link.
  2. A confirmation always appears ("if an account exists…") — this is deliberate and doesn't reveal whether the email is registered.
  3. Open the reset link from your email, which takes you to a page pre-filled with your email and reset token — both shown read-only with a pencil icon to edit them if needed.
  4. Enter and confirm a new password (at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, and a number) — a live checkmark confirms once the two entries match.
  5. Submit. If the link has expired or is invalid, the page explains this and offers a Request a new reset link button back to /forgot-password.

Client Book an appointment

RoleAccess
Guest✗
Client✓
Artist✗
Owner✗
Issuer✓

The client's home screen at /book. Request a new appointment with an artist at your currently active studio, and see your upcoming and past bookings below the form. If you don't belong to a studio yet, you're pointed to Discover instead.

Steps (in field order)

  1. Pick an Artist (searchable dropdown).
  2. Choose a Date & time and a Session length — a live availability check appears once both are set.
  3. If the studio has an active deposit rule, choose it and see the estimated deposit amount.
  4. Add optional Notes (style, size, placement, skin concerns).
  5. Optionally attach up to 6 Reference images (JPEG, PNG, or WebP) — click the box to pick photos, each uploads immediately and shows a thumbnail with a remove (×) button.
  6. Click Request Appointment (disabled while any image is still uploading).
  7. If a deposit is required, you're immediately offered Card or Cash to secure the slot (or you can defer it).
  8. Your existing bookings are listed below the form.
  9. On a Pending or Confirmed booking, click Cancel appointment to self-cancel it — you'll see whether it's a full refund or a partial/forfeited one before confirming, then click Yes, cancel.
  10. Click Reschedule to move the same booking to a new date, time, or duration (only enabled with enough notice — otherwise contact the studio directly).
Tip: arriving from a specific studio's public page (via its Book button) automatically switches your active studio to that one first.
Cancellation policy: cancelling with enough notice (the studio's configured window, 24 hours by default) refunds your deposit in full. Cancelling later than that may only refund part of it, or none, per the studio's settings. The same notice window gates self-reschedule — inside it, the Reschedule button is disabled.
See also: Pay a deposit, My Studios, Appointment statuses

Reference — FAQ

  1. Can a client use the same account at more than one studio? Yes — see My Studios. You switch which studio is "active" and that's the one your booking form and appointment list use.
  2. What happens to my deposit if I cancel? Depends on the studio's policy at that point; the deposit status will show Refunded or Forfeited on the appointment.
  3. Why can't I remove the "Powered by TattooOS" mark? Only plans with branding removal enabled allow this — see Branding.
  4. Can I change my studio's URL later? Only once — after that the slug is locked. See Studio profile.
  5. How do I get a client account without a studio? Sign up directly at client sign up without following a studio's link — you'll pick a studio afterward via Discover.
  6. What's the difference between an intake form and a consent form? Intake forms capture medical/health history; consent forms are a legal digital signature acknowledging the tattoo procedure's risks, generated as a PDF.
  7. Can artists see each other's clients? Yes, within the same studio — client, design, and schedule data is shared studio-wide among artists and owners.
  8. What happens when a studio's trial ends without subscribing? It enters a Grace Period (read-only), then risks losing access entirely if not resolved — see Subscription plans.
  9. Who can suspend a studio? Only the platform team (Issuer role) — see Studio list. This is separate from billing status.
  10. How do referral codes work? A studio shares a link; a new studio that registers with that code gets a free month once it subscribes. See Referral programme.
  11. Can I pay my deposit in cash instead of by card? Yes — the deposit flow always offers both options.
  12. Why does my portfolio photo count for Discover ranking? Photo views are recorded to help rank the Discover feed and profile pages by relevance/popularity.
  13. Can I share a design with someone who doesn't have an account? Yes — use Share a design to generate a 30-day public link.
  14. What if I forget my password? Use Forgot password — a reset link is emailed regardless of whether the account exists, for security.
