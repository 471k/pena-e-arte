# Naming & Positioning Note — Pena e Artë

## The name, as-is

"Pena e Artë" (Albanian, "The Golden Quill") is a strong brand story: craft-rooted, distinctive, ownable as a visual mark (quill icon, gold accent). It is not a strong functional signal — nobody scrolling past an ad guesses "tattoo studio booking software" from either the Albanian phrase or its English translation. It also carries a diacritic (ë) that most keyboards, ad platforms, and search bars mangle, which fragments search traffic and word-of-mouth at global scale.

## Working rule for all copy going forward

- Use **"Pena e Arte"** (no diacritic) as the spelling in every functional surface: domain, social handles, ad copy, App Store listing, email signatures, headers. Treat the ë as a logo/wordmark flourish only, never required for the product to be found or typed.
- Never let the name carry meaning alone. Every first-touch placement (ad, bio, listing title, landing page hero) pairs the name with a plain-English descriptor: *"Pena e Arte — booking & studio management for tattoo shops."*
- "The Golden Quill" / the origin story belongs in brand storytelling (About page, founder note, deck narrative) — not in acquisition copy, where it does no positioning work and adds a beat of confusion.
- Do not translate or explain the Albanian meaning in customer-facing acquisition copy unless the context is explicitly a brand/origin story piece. It's a nice detail, not a selling point for a studio owner deciding whether to switch off Instagram DMs.

## On renaming

Worth naming honestly: the name itself is a bigger lever than most copy decisions, and this one has real drag for a "reach maximum studios worldwide" goal — non-Latin character, no functional cue, translation confusion in Spanish/Portuguese-speaking markets ("Pena de Arte" reads as an unrelated phrase). If global paid acquisition and cold organic discovery (search, App Store, TikTok/IG ads) are the primary growth channel, a rename is worth considering. If growth is mostly referral/word-of-mouth within the tattoo industry where the name's craft connotation and story already resonate, the current name plus disciplined descriptor pairing (above) may be enough.

### Alternative name directions (for consideration, not a final pick)

Naming a company is a founder decision, not something to lock in from a marketing pass — these are directions, not a shortlist to choose from unilaterally:

- **Descriptive + industry-clear**: names that state the category outright (e.g., something built around "studio," "ink," "session," or "book") — highest functional clarity, lowest memorability risk, but more crowded/generic-sounding territory to differentiate in.
- **Coined/ownable + short**: an invented short word (easy to trademark, easy to say in any language, no translation risk) paired with a tagline that does the category-explaining work instead of the name.
- **Keep the craft metaphor, drop the barrier**: retain the "quill/ink/mark" imagery that "Golden Quill" evokes, but in a spelling and word that types cleanly on any keyboard and doesn't require translation to land.

Recommend treating this as a separate scoped exercise — a short naming sprint with 8–10 candidates scored against category clarity, global typability, trademark availability, and domain availability — rather than deciding it inside a copy note. Happy to run that sprint if useful.
