## Brand voice

Direct, no-fluff. Short sentences, benefit-first, minimal adjectives — respect a busy owner's time. Casual-professional: talk like a peer who knows the industry, not corporate SaaS.

Avoid: "synergy," "unlock," "seamless," "revolutionize," exclamation-heavy hype, generic SaaS clichés.

## Audience

Primary: independent and small-chain tattoo studio owners evaluating booking/CRM software.
Secondary: artists who influence their studio's decision to adopt.

Studios currently use Instagram DMs and spreadsheets/paper consent forms — no dedicated tool. No named competitor confirmed yet.

#1 objection: "what we do now works fine" — status-quo bias, not price. Copy should lead with the cost of manual chaos (missed appointments, lost consent forms, no-shows, DMs buried in inbox) rather than price comparisons or feature lists.
